How It Works

Order in four simple steps

No app download required for guests. Just a phone camera and a QR code at the table.

1

Scan the QR Code

Each table has a unique QR code. Guests scan it with their phone camera — no app install needed.

2

Browse & Order

A live digital menu opens instantly. Guests browse, customise, and add items — no rushing, no pressure.

3

Pay Instantly

Pay online via UPI, card, or wallet — or choose to pay at the counter. No awkward waiting for the bill.

4

Kitchen Gets Notified

Orders land directly on the kitchen display the moment they're confirmed. No relay, no errors.
