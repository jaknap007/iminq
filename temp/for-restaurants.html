The Problem

Where restaurants lose time every day

😤

Guests waiting to order

During peak hours, guests wait too long just to get someone's attention. That frustration shows up in reviews.

📝

Order errors & mishearing

Handwritten or verbal orders get misread. Wrong dishes waste kitchen time and disappoint guests.

⏱️

Slow table turnaround

Waiting to order, then waiting for the bill — adds 10–15 unnecessary minutes to every table's stay.

🖨️

Outdated printed menus

Reprinting menus every time a price or item changes is expensive, slow, and easy to forget.
